# 📊 WhatsApp SaaS Startup — Business & Onboarding Summary

## 1. Finalized Pricing & Credit Model

### Pricing Plans (Prepaid, Credit-based)
- **Starter**: ₹99 → 100 conversations included → ₹1.00 per extra conversation
- **Pro**: ₹499 → 500 conversations included → ₹0.75 per extra conversation
- **Business**: ₹1,499 → 2,000 conversations included → ₹0.60 per extra conversation
- **Enterprise**: ₹4,999 → 10,000 conversations included → ₹0.50 per extra conversation

### Key Rules
- Vendors **prepay** monthly subscription (includes credits). 
- Each conversation deducts **1 credit** from plan balance.
- If included credits are exhausted:
  - System auto-switches to **extra conversation billing**.
  - Extra conversations are deducted from **wallet balance** at plan’s extra rate.
- If wallet balance is insufficient → service **pauses until recharge**.
- Auto top-up option for vendors to avoid disruption.

## 2. Revenue & Profitability Projection (New Model)

### Base Assumptions
- Average vendor usage: 120% of plan credits (20% spillover → charged as extra conversations)
- Blended infra/API cost: ~₹0.30 per conversation

📌 *Extra conversations drive significant margin expansion while prepaid model ensures zero bad debt.*

## 3. Vendor Dashboard Flow (Prepaid Credits System)

### Dashboard Overview
1. **Top Bar Quick Stats**
   - Plan: Pro (₹499/month)
   - Credits Left: 120 / 500
   - Wallet Balance: ₹82
   - Renewal Date: Aug 30, 2025

2. **Credits & Usage**
   - Plan Credits: 500
   - Used: 380
   - Remaining: 120
   - Extra Conversations: 40 (₹30 deducted from wallet)
   - Visual progress bar + usage pie chart

3. **Wallet Section**
   - Balance: ₹82
   - Low Balance Alert: “Balance below ₹100, recharge now to avoid service stop.”
   - Options: **Recharge Wallet** / **Enable Auto Top-up**

4. **Plan Management**
   - Current: Pro (₹499, 500 credits)
   - Upgrade/Downgrade Options → Starter / Business / Enterprise
   - Switching plan adjusts credits instantly.

5. **Usage Timeline**
   - Graph: Conversations per day for last 30 days
   - Highlights extra usage days (wallet deductions)

### Flow When Vendor Stops Paying
1. Wallet < renewal fee → Plan suspended.
2. Dashboard greys out except **Recharge Wallet** button.
3. On recharge, service resumes instantly.

- **Predictable Revenue**: Prepaid = no overdue invoices.
- **Scalable**: Extra conversation charges ensure high ARPU from active vendors.
- **Flexible**: Vendors can start small and scale up seamlessly.
- **Investor-Friendly**: Transparent unit economics, strong margins at scale.

📌 With this **credit-based prepaid model**, we balance **low entry pricing, profitability, and zero credit risk** — making it ideal for India’s MSME ecosystem.
