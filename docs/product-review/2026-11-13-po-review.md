## 3. Market changes since the last review

The baseline is [`docs/PORTAL_IMPROVEMENT_AND_COMPETITIVE_REVIEW.md`](../PORTAL_IMPROVEMENT_AND_COMPETITIVE_REVIEW.md), dated 12 Nov 2026. It compares ChatLogix with Wati, AiSensy, Interakt, Gallabox, LimeChat, Zoko, and DoubleTick. It does not mention Meta. That is the change.

**1. Meta ships its own assistant to the same shop.** Meta announced Business AI inside the WhatsApp Business app for Indian small businesses in May 2026. Meta's page says it answers frequently asked questions (pricing, discounts, shipping), recommends products from the shop's profile and catalog, captures leads, books appointments, and lets the owner take over a chat. It says the feature is available "in all native Indian languages" and that UPI payment inside the chat is "starting soon". The page gives no price, lists no eligibility criteria, and does not describe a cart, an order record, an order-status answer, or voice notes. It names only the WhatsApp Business app. It says nothing about numbers on the Business Platform (the API), which is where a ChatLogix shop's number lives. Indian press also reported a "Meta Business Agent" in July 2026. That article could not be read, so nothing here relies on it.

What it changes:

- "An assistant that answers customers in their language" is no longer something only a paid tool offers a one-owner shop. Wati or AiSensy at ₹1,350 a month was the comparison on 12 Nov. A tool already inside the owner's phone is the comparison now.
- What Meta's page does not claim is what ChatLogix already does: a cart that becomes an order, an order the owner can see and mark paid, an answer to "where is my order" from the order row, and voice notes. Tasks 3, 4, 6, and 9 are the work that keeps that difference real. Their place in Phase 2 is right.
- Tasks 8 and 11 (take over the chat, quick replies) are now things a shop sees in the WhatsApp Business app itself. They are parity, and they stay where they are.
- WhatsApp catalog cards is the Phase 4 note most exposed. Meta's assistant recommends from the WhatsApp catalog. That note is now marked first in line when Phase 4 opens. It still waits for Phase 3's exit.
- The positioning itself is an owner decision. It is open decision 2.

**2. Meta bills per message, not per conversation.** Meta moved the Business Platform from conversation pricing to per-message pricing on 1 July 2025 and changes rates only on the first day of a quarter. Third-party pages put India at about ₹0.86 for a marketing template and about ₹0.115 for a utility template. Treat those as direction and read Meta's rate card before using them. Consequences: the per-turn platform fee can stay 0 after task 1, because a free-form reply inside the 24-hour window is not a template send. The Phase 4 utility-template note will need a per-message price, not a per-conversation one. `CLAUDE.md` and [`docs/BILLING_AND_PRICING.md`](../BILLING_AND_PRICING.md) still say "per-conversation fees". That wording is out of date. It was not edited in this review because both files are outside the scope.

**3. The paid field has not moved in a way that changes the plan.** Comparison pages from May to August 2026 still show seats, an AI add-on (AiSensy about ₹1,350 a month), and a markup on Meta's fees. Several of those pages are written by competing vendors, and they disagree with each other on Wati's and Gallabox's plans. None of it changes a priority. Two leads were seen and not checked: Shiprocket's Shunya.ai at a reported ₹499 a month for voice, text, and image in Indian languages, and a Zoko case study on AI order taking. Check both at the next review.

## 8. Risks

- **Price.** Until task 1's note exists, nobody knows what the next reply costs a shop. Every later check that sends a live reply waits on it.
- **Shared Redis.** If a development process shares production Redis, its settings writes price production turns, and a key delete does not hold. Task 1 now stops and escalates in that case.
- **Tenant isolation.** An admin who is also a vendor can save to the wrong shop from the inspection screen today. Do not edit catalogs through admin inspection until task 2's test is green.
- **Not verifiable from the repo.** The live `PlatformSettings` row and Redis key, Lambda environment variables, Ibell's `WhatsAppNumber` rows, Lumina's registration row, the five open queries, and the saved VPA and payee for Ibell.
- **External waits.** Razorpay's written confirmation for D1 (live Route). Not needed for Phases 1 to 3.
- **Meta.** Business AI's price, eligibility, and rollout were not stated on Meta's page. If it is free and reaches Tamil Nadu shops, the cost argument against Wati and AiSensy stops being the one that matters.
