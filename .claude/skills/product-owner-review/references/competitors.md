## Newer or adjacent threats to scan on each refresh

Check, and only cite what you can source:
- Meta Business AI (now in the table above): what changed since the last read. Price, eligibility, whether UPI in chat shipped, whether it takes an order, whether it works on API numbers. A separate "Meta Business Agent" was reported in July 2026 and was not verified.
- Meta bills the Business Platform per message since 1 July 2025 (not per conversation) and changes rates on the first day of a quarter. Read the live India rate card before quoting a template price.
- Shiprocket's Shunya.ai (reported ₹499/month, voice/text/image in Indian languages) and Zoko's AI order-taking case study. Seen 2026-11-13, not verified.
- Shopify Inbox / Shopify's WhatsApp tooling for Indian sellers.
- Indian BSPs adding a cheap "AI replies" tier aimed at home businesses (Gupshup, Kaleyra, BiteSpeed, Kwik and similar).
- Changes to Meta's India rate card (service vs utility vs marketing conversations) that move the cost gap.

## What to compare, per requirement

Ask only the questions that bear on the requirement in front of you:
- **Ordering and language:** natural-language ordering in Tamil/Hindi/Tanglish, voice notes.
- **Inbox:** human handoff, quick/canned replies, internal notes, assignment, second agent.
- **Orders:** order-status answers (WISMO), status notifications, cancelled/empty-order handling.
- **Payments:** in-chat payment links, UPI/WhatsApp Pay status written back to the order, COD confirmation.
- **Catalog:** catalog cards / multi-product messages, sync vs manual entry.
- **Commercials:** monthly plan vs usage, AI add-on price, Meta fee markup, whether model cost is shown to the shop.
- **Onboarding:** time to connect a number, BSP status visibility, approval.

## Positioning guardrail

ChatLogix should not become a cheaper Wati. Broadcasts, click-to-WhatsApp ads, Shopify/WooCommerce sync, an omnichannel inbox, AI voice calls and seat pricing are on the launch "Will not build" list. Competitor evidence can argue for moving one of these, but that is an **Open decision** for the owner, never an edit you make on your own.
